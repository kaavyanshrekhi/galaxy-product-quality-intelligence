## Key Insights

- Battery-related issues are the biggest complaint driver.
- Resolution performance varies across service centers.
- Quality risk should be prioritized, not just complaint volume.
- Battery and charging problems dominate customer complaints.
- August recorded the highest number of complaints among all months.

## Business Recommendations

1. Prioritize Battery & Overheating Issues
2. Improve Underperforming Service Centers
3. Use Quality Risk Score for Prioritization
